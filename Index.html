About Dr. Norman Borlaug

Dr. Norman Borlaug was an American agronomist who helped develop high-yield, disease-resistant wheat varieties. His work helped increase food production and fight hunger around the world.

He is widely known as the father of the Green Revolution and received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1970 for his contributions to global food security.

Timeline of His Life

  • 1914: Born on March 25 in Iowa, United States.
  • 1937: Graduated from the University of Minnesota.
  • 1944: Began agricultural research in Mexico.
  • 1960s: His improved wheat varieties helped increase food production in several countries.
  • 1970: Received the Nobel Peace Prize.
  • 2006: Received the Congressional Gold Medal.
  • 2009: Passed away on September 12, leaving a lasting legacy in agriculture.

His Legacy

Dr. Borlaug's work continues to inspire scientists, farmers, and researchers to find better ways to feed the world's growing population.
